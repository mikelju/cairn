- Increment 11 before 14: the highlights of recorded quotations can only be generated after the recordings, so increment 14 re-runs the build; alternatively 11 could move after 14, at the cost of testing highlights later.
- Increment 4 (weights) is placed after the passages because passages need no model; it could go first if the CI cache step should be proven early.
